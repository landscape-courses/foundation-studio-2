## Decided
- The site walk follows Lizzie's lecture on W9 Thu 3/25: 2.0 Score is assigned there and students
  walk the avenue that afternoon; 2.0 is due W10 Mon 3/29, when 2.1 Forest is assigned. Studies are
  assigned and due on Mondays, checked in on Thursdays. Weeks count from W1 (Thu 1/28), recess
  included. (2026-11-27, Zihao)
- 2.1 Forest: where the sections differ in size, the leftover student makes a team of three, which
  takes the topic with the most work. Graded as a team, each member the same grade. (2026-11-27, Zihao)
- 2.1 Forest is a team study: ten ecology topics, and the two students on the same topic in the two
  sections form a team that coordinates across sections. One week of research, then the next week
  ten team presentations, pecha kucha style: 10 to 15 slides at 30 seconds each, 5 to 7 minutes
  per team, both sections together. (2026-11-27, Zihao)
- P2 is specific to movement and trees. Students study planted form as a lexicon, like P1's
  landform terms: each takes one or two forms and draws the space they make. One week of forest
  ecology research by topic, asking what forest ecology means for an urban forest. (2026-11-27, Zihao)
- P2 carries two agendas: movement on the avenue and the urban forest. The score is one
  Halprin-style mapping study, not the spine of P2. (2026-11-27, Zihao)
- S15 (Thu 3/25) is the P2 site intro: a lecture by Lizzie MacWillie, Assistant Director of the
  J. Max Bond Center for Urban Futures, on her work with community partners on St. Nicholas Ave.
  P2 is a real, partner-funded project and the partner wants to see ideas from the studio; Lizzie
  joins some reviews later. P2 follows P1's logic: small studies lead to the final, named and
  structured like P1 (2.0 ungraded warm-up, 2.1 to 2.5 graded). (2026-11-27, Zihao)
- The site model is a team collaboration exercise per section of 10, run like a firm on a job, in
  parallel with the studies on the model shop's schedule, done before 1.4 Scale is assigned (S09,
  Mon 3/1). Crews: layout (nests the laser templates onto sheets), cut (cuts, labels, checks and
  bags pieces by layer), glue, void and insert template, and one project manager; each student
  serves on two crews. A shared
  timesheet logs 15-minute entries by task code, budget against actual, and a plate on the base
  credits each name with its hours. Those with the fewest hours take the final assembly. Every handoff carries a transmittal: a labeled bag by layer,
  a checklist, the receiver's signature. Ungraded. (2026-11-25, Zihao)
- 1.4 Scale is a section model: each student laser-cuts one strip of the park at 1" = 20', 1 ft
  contours, one board per layer. The strip runs from Broadway to the street on the east, about
  410 x 117 ft (20.5" x 6", one piece on the laser bed). Its south face is the student's design
  section and matches their section drawing; the north side meets the existing park and the short
  ends meet the street elevations. About 80 layers. This replaces the void and the clay insert.
  1.5 Proposal draws it: contour plan and long section at 1" = 20', and the perspective section
  collage. (2026-11-25, Zihao)
- P1 midterm is a science fair with external guests at S14 (Mon 3/22); no separate S12 pin-up.
  1.4 Scale, the section model, runs one week (3/1 to 3/8); 1.5 Proposal, the drawings,
  runs two weeks (3/8 to 3/22). (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- Grading: ten graded studies at 9% each (90%), five per project, 1.0 an ungraded warm-up;
  midterm, the S14 science fair, 5%; final review 5%. The site model is a team build, ungraded.
  No participation grade. (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- S03 workshop is Grasshopper (lofting key sections) for the catalog; the contour workshop is
  dropped, since students already read contours. (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- P1 drawings are spread across the studies. 1.3 checks the chosen model at body scale, the space
  a body would occupy, with plan enlargements and sections at 1" = 8'. (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- P1 has a catalog study after the warm-up: a simple Grasshopper script lofts key sections of
  each of the three terms, developed into at least 12 isometrics on an 11x17 sheet, 4 per term,
  abstract and without site, to explore the limits of each landform. The clay iterations start
  from one catalog item. (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- P1 students move between models and drawings. The P1 drawing set at the end: a contour plan,
  plan enlargements, sections at several scales, and a perspective section collage.
  (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- P1 studies are landform model-making; the site is a testing ground, not a problem to solve.
  (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- 1.0 warm-up, in S01 (Thu 1/28): a scavenger hunt. Students get a list of landform terms, search
  the school for materials, and make a set of small 4" x 4" study models in class, one term each.
  (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- Assignments are small studies of one to two weeks, so each week students make something new.
  A study is an inquiry rather than a task to finish. Each study has its own page, listed in the
  left nav under a P1 section and a P2 section; the site is the single source for the course.
  (2026-11-24, Zihao)
- Two projects of 14 sessions each, sharing one spine: movement and choreography, A to B. Each
