STUDY 1.2
Clay
One catalog item · three iterations in clay
AssignedMon 2/8
Check-inThu 2/11
DueThu 2/18
OutcomeThree clay iterations of one form
Learning Objectives
  • Iterative design through rule-based form-making
  • Translating a rule into a clay form, and revising the rule from what the form does
AssignmentThree clay models of one item from your catalog, each a revision of the one before, photographed and noted.
Choose the catalog item you most want to keep working on. From here on you carry one form to the midterm.
Clay answers back. It slumps, holds an edge, keeps a fingerprint. Let each iteration respond to what the last one showed you.
01 Choose One Item
Pick one of the twelve isometrics. Say in a sentence why this one.
02 First Iteration
Model it in clay on a 6″ × 6″ base, as close to the isometric as you can.
03 Two More
Make a second and a third. Each one changes the decision the last one made you doubt.
04 Photograph and Note
Photograph all three from the same angle and write one line under each: what changed and why.
Due. Due Thursday because the college is closed Mon 2/15. The section site model is built in class alongside this study (S04 to S06).
