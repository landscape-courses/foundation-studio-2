AssignedMon 2/22

OutcomeEnlargements, sections, revised model
Learning Objectives
  • Reading a model at the scale of a body
  • Using plan and section to test a form
  • Revising the model from what the drawings show
AssignmentPlan enlargements and at least three sections at 1″ = 8′ of one clay iteration, with figures, followed by a revised clay model.
Until now the form has had no size. This week you give it one and ask what kind of space it makes for a body: where you would climb, stop, sit, look out or feel enclosed.
Draw first. The sections show details the model hides, and the model is revised from them.
01 Give It a Size
Pick one of your three clay iterations and decide how large it is in feet, so a body can enter it.
02 Cut Sections
Draw at least three sections through the places a body would move, at 1″ = 8′, with figures.
03 Enlarge the Plan
Draw plan enlargements of two or three places where a body would pause, at the same scale.
04 Revise the Model
Rework the clay so it holds the spaces the drawings found. Photograph before and after.
Due. Pin up the drawings and bring both models, before and after, on Monday.
