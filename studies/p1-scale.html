STUDY 1.4
Scale
An 8″ clay model · the void · a test-fit in the site model
AssignedMon 3/1
Check-inThu 3/4
DueMon 3/8
Outcome8″ × 8″ clay insert
Learning Objectives
  • Scale-dependent decisions: each scale frames its own questions
  • Site fit and grading at 1:500, circulation and edges at 1:100, material and fabrication at 1:20
AssignmentYour form built larger in clay, as an 8″ × 8″ insert that fits the void of the site model at 1″ = 20′, tested in both section site models.
Amelia Gorman Park is the testing ground. The site model has an 8″ × 8″ square void, rotated to the park; at 1″ = 20′ that is 160 feet a side. Your form now has to meet the ground around it.
Build it, fit it, and revise it where the ground pushes back.
01 Fit the Void
Use the edge template so the insert matches the void outline, meets the boundary heights and sits on the base datum.
02 Build Bigger
Model the insert in clay, 8″ × 8″, carried up from your 1.3 model.
03 Test-Fit
Set the insert in your section’s site model at the check-in. Photograph it in place from the Broadway and Wadsworth edges.
04 Revise
Rework the insert where it fights the ground around it, then fit it again.
Due. Bring the insert, fitted to the site model, on Mon 3/8.
