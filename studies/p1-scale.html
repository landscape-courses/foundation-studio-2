STUDY 1.4
Scale
A larger model · the void · contour plan and long section at 1″ = 20′
AssignedMon 3/1
Check-inThu 3/4 · Mon 3/8
DueMon 3/15
OutcomeInsert, contour plan, long section
Learning Objectives
  • Moving a form between scales
  • Drawing contours while building, so model and plan stay one
  • Testing a form against a real ground
AssignmentYour form built larger as an insert that fits the void of the site model at 1″ = 20′, drawn as a site contour plan and a long section at the same scale.
Amelia Gorman Park is the testing ground. The site model has an 8″ void, rotated to the hill; at 1″ = 20′ that is 160 feet a side. Your form now has to meet the ground around it.
Build and draw at the same time. Each contour you cut or carve is a line on the plan.
01 Fit the Void
Use the edge template so the insert matches the void outline, meets the boundary heights and sits on the base datum.
02 Build Bigger
Make the insert in the material that suits your form: 3D print, laser cut or plasticine. Test it in the section’s site model.
03 Draw the Contour Plan
Draw the site contour plan with your insert at 1″ = 20′, as you build.
04 Draw the Long Section
Cut a long section through your insert from Broadway to Wadsworth Terrace at 1″ = 20′.
Due. This study runs two weeks, so the contours can be drawn with care as the insert is built. Bring the insert and pin up both drawings on Mon 3/15.
