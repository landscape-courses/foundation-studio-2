STUDY 1.1
Catalog
Key sections · a Grasshopper loft · twelve isometrics
AssignedMon 2/1
Check-inThu 2/4, Grasshopper workshop
DueMon 2/8
Outcome12 isometrics on one 11×17 sheet
Learning Objectives
  • Describing a landform by its key sections
  • Changing one parameter to find where a form stops being itself
  • Laying out a catalog so differences read at a glance
AssignmentAn 11×17 catalog of at least twelve isometrics, four for each of your three terms, lofted in Grasshopper from key sections. Abstract, with no site.
A landform can be described by a few sections through it. Loft between them and the form appears; change one section and the whole form changes. This study uses that to test how far each of your terms can go.
The catalog has no site. It explores the limits of each landform on its own terms.
01 Draw Key Sections
For each of your three terms, draw three to five sections across the form, taken from your 1.0 model. Bring them to the check-in.
02 Loft in Grasshopper
At the S03 workshop you build a short script: section curves in, a loft out. Run it on all three terms.
03 Push Each Term Four Ways
Make four variations of each term. Change one thing at a time: depth, width, spacing of the sections, steepness, twist. Push the fourth until it nearly stops being that term.
04 Lay Out the Sheet
Three rows, one per term, four isometrics in each row, all at the same view and scale. Label each with the term and the one thing that changed.
Due. Submit the 11×17 sheet as a PDF by Monday and pin it up in studio.
