STUDY 1.5
Insert
The final insert · a perspective section collage · the midterm set
AssignedMon 3/8
Check-in— (no studio Thu 3/11)
DueMon 3/15, pin-up
OutcomeFinal insert, perspective section collage
Learning Objectives
  • Resolving a form into one built proposal
  • Showing how the space feels to a body moving through it
  • Presenting a body of work as one argument
AssignmentThe final insert, fitted to both site models, and a perspective section collage of one moment along the climb.
This week the form becomes a proposal. Finish the insert and make one drawing that puts the viewer inside it.
After the pin-up you have one week of revision. The midterm shows the whole run of studies together.
01 Finish the Insert
Resolve the insert so it fits either section’s site model. Test-fit it in both before the pin-up.
02 Make the Collage
Choose one moment along the climb. Draw it as a perspective section, then collage in material, light and people.
03 Pin Up
At the S12 science fair both sections hang their work in one room. Bring the insert in its model.
04 Revise for the Midterm
From the pin-up to Mon 3/22, revise. The midterm set is the work of every study: the catalog, the clay iterations, the enlargements and sections at 1″ = 8′, the contour plan and long section at 1″ = 20′, the collage and the final insert.
Due. Midterm review Mon 3/22, with guest critics.
