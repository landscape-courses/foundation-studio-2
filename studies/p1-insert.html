STUDY 1.5
Insert
The final insert · a perspective section collage · the midterm set
AssignedMon 3/15
Check-inThu 3/18
DueMon 3/22, midterm science fair
OutcomeFinal insert, perspective section collage
Learning Objectives
  • Resolving a form into one built proposal
  • Showing how the space feels to a body moving through it
  • Presenting a body of work as one argument
AssignmentThe final insert, fitted to both site models, and a perspective section collage of one moment along the climb.
This week the form becomes a proposal. Finish the insert and make one drawing that puts the viewer inside it.
The midterm is a science fair with external guests, and it shows the whole run of studies together.
01 Finish the Insert
Resolve the insert so it fits either section’s site model. Test-fit it in both before the science fair.
02 Make the Collage
Choose one moment along the climb. Draw it as a perspective section, then collage in material, light and people.
03 Pin Up
Test-fit the insert in both site models at the Thursday check-in, then revise.
04 Hang the Midterm
At the S14 science fair both sections hang their work in one room, inserts in the models, and external guests circulate. The midterm set is the work of every study: the catalog, the clay iterations, the enlargements and sections at 1″ = 8′, the contour plan and long section at 1″ = 20′, the collage and the final insert.
Due. Midterm science fair Mon 3/22 (S14), with external guests.
